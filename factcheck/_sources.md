# Source registry — deprecated (2026-07)

**Policy change (author decision):** external references are maintained in a
`## References (external)` section at the bottom of each factcheck mirror
file, next to the cards that cite them — a single central registry proved
heavy to maintain and dangerous to trust (bundled records, silent staleness).
A registry model may return later; until then, do not add entries here.

The entries that lived in this file have been distributed to the mirror
files that cite them (landmark algorithms, cryptography, networking, QEC,
modern frontier, QML, and the Appendix F mirror). Anything not distributed
was either unused or is covered by inline citations in the manuscript.
