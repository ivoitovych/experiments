## Findings

### 1. Moving-target warning coverage is still incomplete

The project now has a good policy for perishable claims: visible `Moving-target warning` callouts in draft text, plus a lint gate preventing such warnings from shipping in `final` files. The policy is working mechanically.

However, the current inventory of warning callouts appears narrower than the manuscript's actual set of current/time-sensitive claims. Several chapters without a callout contain specific 2025/2026 hardware, standards, deployment, or benchmark statements that are at least as perishable as the sections already marked.

Representative examples:

- `book/part-09-hardware-and-software/20-quantum-hardware-platforms.md`
  - The whole chapter is explicitly a 2026 platform survey. It names processors, fidelities, qubit counts, modalities, and vendor trajectories, but has no visible moving-target warning.
- `book/part-08-noise-and-qec/18-noise-decoherence-and-errors.md`
  - Contains practical mid-2025 coherence and fidelity numbers, plus "current devices" benchmark claims.
- `book/part-08-noise-and-qec/19-quantum-error-correction-and-fault-tolerance.md`
  - Contains 2025-2026 distance-scaling results, physical-to-logical overhead estimates, Google/IBM/qLDPC claims, and RSA-2048 resource anchors.
- `book/part-11-applications/27-cryptography-and-security.md`
  - Contains current PQC deployment timelines, NIST/FIPS status, OpenSSL/OpenSSH/browser/mobile deployment claims, Falcon/FN-DSA/HQC timelines, and HNDL guidance.
- `book/part-11-applications/31-quantum-sensing-metrology-and-tomography.md`
  - Contains deployed sensing status, clock uncertainties, NV/SQUID/OPM/gravimeter performance numbers, and commercial/clinical deployment claims.
- `book/part-12-adjacent-models/33-quantum-communication-and-networking.md`
  - Contains satellite-QKD mission status, operational service claims, twin-field distance records, and active simulator/tooling claims.
- `book/part-13-perspective-and-direction/37-endgame.md`
  - Contains current hardware calibration numbers, logical-qubit status, and quantum-advantage status summaries.

Recommendation: either add visible moving-target warning callouts to these chapters/sections too, or document a narrower rule explaining why the existing eight warnings are the only publication-gated perishable sections. As written, the policy is good but unevenly applied.
